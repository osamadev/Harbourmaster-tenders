### 1. Scope of Works
The Contractor shall provide pavement maintenance and resurfacing services
across the Northern District for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months.

### 2. Variation of Scope
The Company may, at its sole discretion and without prior notice, vary the
scope, volume, or location of works by any amount. The Contractor shall absorb
all costs arising from such variations within the agreed contract price.

### 3. Liability and Indemnity
The Contractor shall indemnify the Company against any and all losses,
damages, claims, and liabilities of any nature whatsoever, without limitation
as to amount, arising directly or indirectly from the performance of this
contract, including consequential and indirect losses.

### 4. Payment Terms
The Company shall settle valid, undisputed invoices within ninety (90) days
of receipt. Interest shall not be payable on late payment under any
circumstances.

### 5. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property created, developed, or used by the Contractor in
connection with the works — including pre-existing background IP — shall vest
absolutely and irrevocably in the Company upon creation.

### 6. Termination
The Company may terminate this contract for convenience at any time on seven
(7) days' written notice. The Contractor shall have no equivalent right of
termination and shall not be entitled to compensation for early termination.

### 7. Service Levels
The Contractor shall perform the works "to a high standard and in a timely
manner." Specific performance metrics, response times, and acceptance criteria
are not defined in this document and will be determined later by the Company.

### 8. Subcontracting
The Contractor may subcontract any portion of the works without the Company's
consent, provided the Contractor remains liable for subcontractor performance.

### 9. Governing Law
This contract shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the
Company is established.
