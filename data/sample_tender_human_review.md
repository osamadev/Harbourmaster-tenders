### 1. Scope of Works
The Supplier shall design, build, host, operate, and maintain a nationwide
harbour access control platform covering vessel permits, gate automation,
identity verification, berth scheduling, and real-time incident reporting.

### 2. Unlimited Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Company, its officers, contractors, and
agents against all claims, damages, regulatory penalties, third-party losses,
cybersecurity incidents, data breaches, business interruption, consequential
loss, and reputational harm arising directly or indirectly from this contract,
without financial cap or limitation.

### 3. Critical Delivery Deadline
The full production platform must be live across all participating ports within
forty-five (45) calendar days of contract signature. Time is of the essence. Any
delay, regardless of cause, will incur liquidated damages of GBP 250,000 per day.
No extension of time will be available for dependency delays, third-party
integration failures, severe weather, industrial action, or Company-side
approval delays.

### 4. Payment and Acceptance
Payment will be made only after final acceptance of the complete national
platform. The Company may withhold acceptance at its sole discretion if any
module, integration, performance target, security test, or user experience
requirement is unsatisfactory. No milestone payments, mobilisation fees, or
partial acceptance payments are available.

### 5. Background Intellectual Property
All software, algorithms, models, configuration assets, documentation, tools,
templates, and background intellectual property used or adapted by the Supplier
for the project shall transfer irrevocably to the Company at no additional
cost, including the right to sublicense to any public-sector body.

### 6. Security and Data Obligations
The Supplier will be solely responsible for all security incidents and data
protection failures affecting the platform, including failures caused by
Company personnel, port operators, legacy systems, third-party APIs, or users.
The Supplier must notify all affected parties and regulators within one (1) hour
of detection and bear all remediation, compensation, investigation, and legal
costs.

### 7. Termination for Convenience
The Company may terminate for convenience on three (3) days' written notice.
Upon termination, the Supplier shall provide full transition assistance for up
to twelve (12) months at no additional charge and shall not be entitled to lost
profit, demobilisation costs, or recovery of unrecovered implementation costs.

### 8. Change Control
The Company may add ports, integrations, identity providers, analytics
features, or reporting requirements at any time. Such changes will be treated as
included in the fixed contract price unless the Company expressly agrees in
writing that a separate fee is payable.

### 9. Insurance
The Supplier must maintain professional indemnity, cyber, public liability, and
employer's liability insurance at levels determined by the Company from time
to time. Failure to obtain any revised insurance level within five (5) business
days will constitute a material breach.

### 10. Governing Law
This contract shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
