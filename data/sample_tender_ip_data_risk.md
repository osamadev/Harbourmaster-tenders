processing personal and special-category data for the Authority.

protection terms, and the Authority may mandate sub-processors of its choosing.

### 8. Retention and Deletion
The Supplier must retain all data indefinitely and simultaneously delete it on demand within
one hour, certifying complete destruction across all backups.

### 9. IP Indemnity Without Cap

claims without financial limit, including claims arising from Authority instructions.

### 10. Audit

notice and copy any materials it considers relevant.
