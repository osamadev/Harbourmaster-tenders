### 1. Scope of Works
The Supplier shall provide a managed payments reconciliation service across the Company's
twelve regional offices for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months.

### 2. Payment on Final Acceptance Only
No payment of any kind shall be made until final acceptance of the entire service. No
milestone payments, mobilisation fees, advance payments, or partial acceptances are
available, and acceptance may be withheld at the Company's sole and absolute discretion.

### 3. Punitive Liquidated Damages
Any failure to meet a service level shall incur liquidated damages of 10% of the total
contract value per occurrence, uncapped in aggregate, deductible immediately from any future
sums and recoverable as a debt regardless of actual loss.

### 4. Fixed Price Despite Unlimited Change
The contract price is fixed for the full term. The Company may add offices, transaction
volumes, and reporting requirements without limit, all deemed included in the fixed price
unless the Company expressly agrees a separate fee in writing.

### 5. Retention
The Company shall retain 30% of every invoice as security for the full term plus a 12-month
defects period, released only after a further clean audit at the Supplier's cost.

### 6. Currency and Inflation
All prices are fixed in a currency to be nominated by the Company at each payment date, and
the Supplier bears all foreign-exchange and inflation risk over the term.

### 7. Set-off
The Company may set off against any invoice any amount it considers, in its sole judgement,
to be owed by the Supplier under this or any other contract between the parties.

### 8. Audit and Clawback
The Company may audit the Supplier's pricing at any time for seven years and claw back any
amount it deems excessive, with interest, without independent determination.

### 9. Insurance at Company Discretion
The Supplier shall maintain insurance at levels set by the Company from time to time, and any
increase must be obtained within five business days or the contract is in material breach.

### 10. No Recovery on Termination
On termination for any reason, the Supplier forfeits all unpaid sums, retention, and
unrecovered implementation costs, and shall not be entitled to lost profit or demobilisation
costs.
