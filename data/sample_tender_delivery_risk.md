## National Sensor Rollout Programme (Synthetic Sample)

### 1. Scope of Works
The Supplier shall design, manufacture, install, and commission 40,000 environmental sensors
across 600 sites nationwide, including civil works, connectivity, and integration.

### 2. Critical Delivery Deadline
The full programme must be live across all 600 sites within thirty (30) calendar days of
contract signature. Time is of the essence in every respect.

### 3. No Extension of Time
No extension of time shall be granted for any cause whatsoever, including force majeure,
severe weather, pandemics, industrial action, supply-chain failure, third-party integration
delays, permitting delays, or Company-caused delays.

### 4. Dependency Risk Transferred
The Supplier is solely responsible for all third-party dependencies — power, telecoms,
landlord access, and legacy system availability — and bears all delay risk even where the
dependency is controlled by the Company or a Company nominee.

### 5. Liquidated Damages for Delay
Any delay, regardless of cause, incurs liquidated damages of GBP 100,000 per site per day,
with no cap and no requirement for the Company to demonstrate loss.

### 6. Acceptance Testing
Acceptance requires zero defects of any severity across all 600 sites simultaneously within a
single 24-hour window; any single failure restarts the entire acceptance process.

### 7. Concurrent Milestones
All design, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning milestones must complete in
parallel; sequential delivery is not permitted and does not excuse the deadline.

### 8. Resource Commitment
The Supplier shall maintain dedicated 24/7 crews at every site for the full term at its own
cost, irrespective of whether work is available.

### 9. Change at Will
The Company may relocate sites, change sensor specifications, and add sites at any time
without adjusting the deadline or price.

### 10. Termination for Single Slip
The Company may terminate immediately and recover all costs if any single site misses any
interim date by even one hour.
