### 1. Scope of Works
The Supplier shall provide integrated facilities management — cleaning, security, catering,
and maintenance — across the Company's national estate for thirty-six (36) months.

### 2. Delegation of Authority Override
The signing manager may approve unlimited variations, additional spend, and contract
extensions without finance, legal, or board approval, notwithstanding any internal
delegation-of-authority limit.

### 3. Single-Source Award
This framework is awarded without competition, and the Supplier may be appointed to future
related contracts directly, without tender, for the life of the relationship.

### 4. Subcontracting Without Checks
The Supplier may subcontract any portion of the works to any party without due-diligence,
right-to-work, modern-slavery, or anti-bribery screening, and without Company consent.

### 5. Anti-Bribery and Hospitality
The Supplier and Company personnel may exchange gifts and hospitality of any value, and such
exchanges need not be recorded in any register.

### 6. Insurance Floor Waived
The minimum insurance levels mandated by Company policy are waived; the Supplier may carry
whatever cover, if any, it chooses.

### 7. Conflict of Interest
Company evaluators with a personal or financial interest in the Supplier may participate in
award and performance decisions without declaration or recusal.

### 8. Health, Safety, and Environmental
Statutory health, safety, and environmental obligations are deemed satisfied by the
Supplier's self-certification, with no audit right for the Company.

### 9. Data Protection Compliance
Compliance with applicable data-protection law is optional where the Supplier considers it
commercially impractical.

### 10. Record Keeping
Neither party is required to retain records of decisions, approvals, or payments under this
framework.
